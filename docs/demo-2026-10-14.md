# Demo run sheet, 14 October 2026

Everything below runs locally on the mock provider: every flow works end to
end and costs nothing, and the copy it writes is formula text on purpose. Say
so once, up front ("the writing you'll see is a stand-in; the real model is a
switch in settings"), then never apologise for it again. The "Mock AI" badge
in the editor header says the same thing.

## Before you start

```bash
npm run db:up && npm run db:migrate && npm run db:seed   # once
npm run dev                                               # http://localhost:3000
```

Three accounts are on the sign-in page in dev. Keep two browser windows open:
one signed in as **admin@huemen.studio**, one as **demo@client.test**. Use a
third, private window for **fresh@client.test** when you reach onboarding.

Use **Light** theme for the projector; switch to **Dark** once, from the
sidebar, to show it is a first-class control. Page transitions are deliberately
slow, so let them finish.

## The order (about 20 minutes)

1. **Landing and sign-in** (30s). Passwordless, invite-only. Show the "check
   your email" page, then sign in with the dev button.
2. **Onboarding, as the fresh client** (5 min). Step 0 About you, step 1 paste
   three or four LinkedIn posts (or upload the LinkedIn export), watch
   "Reading your writing". Step 2: the pre-filled answers with their reasons,
   "this or that", the live preview changing as you answer. Step 3 hue: upload
   a logo and watch the palette suggest itself. Step 4: the first piece and
   its voice check. Land on the dashboard. The point: nobody typed a brand
   brief into a prompt.
3. **Brand home, as the demo client** (2 min). The core card and confidence,
   three suggested topics, recent pieces with scores, what's planned, usage
   meter. Click a suggested topic: it opens a new piece at Ideate.
4. **A piece: Ideate, Content, Visual** (5 min). Draft two variants; show a
   steer ("more contrarian"); open History and bring a variant back; the
   never-word flag on the seeded off-brand draft ("flagged, not removed: your
   call"). Double-click the copy on the canvas to edit it in place; Cmd+Z.
   Generate visuals; pick a scheme; **download the post image** and open the
   file (1080x1350, real text). Download the set as a zip.
   **The design-tool moments** (3 min, worth slowing down for):
   - Press **Draft**, give a topic, and watch both versions being written on
     the canvas, word by word. It opens the piece when it finishes.
   - **Click** a hook on the canvas (once, not twice) and type "make it
     punchier", or press a chip. It rewrites in place. Cmd+Z brings it back.
   - On **Visual**, press **Show me options**: four post layouts side by side
     in the client's own words. Keep one. Switch to Carousel and do the same.
   - In **Design this piece**, drag **Type size**, flip **Centre**, **Tint**,
     **Bold**: it redraws as you move. Then download the PNG to show the
     tweak is in the file.
   - Point out the cursor: arrow, hand over buttons, I-beam over text, and
     the small buttons lean toward it. Mac-style, on purpose.
5. **Check** (1 min). Paste anything, pick LinkedIn, the band and the findings
   that quote the draft; "Rewrite in my voice".
6. **Plan** (2 min). Calendar: **Generate 90 days** (pick the cadence and
   channels, it says how many slots before it runs); hover a day and press +.
   A slot's "Start" opens a piece. Ideas: paste one, watch it auto-tag; convert.
7. **Export** (1 min). PDF and Markdown from the project's Export step; open
   the PDF: cover, brief, voice, pillars, calendar, pieces, no vendor marks.
8. **Admin, as the owner** (3 min). Workspaces & access: create a workspace,
   invite someone (the link is there to copy), assign a coach, set monthly AI
   caps. AI usage across workspaces. **Prompts**: edit the LinkedIn post
   template and save; it is live for the next draft, versioned, per workspace
   if wanted. Switch into the demo client's workspace from the sidebar as the
   admin: same studio, their data only.

## What to say if asked

- **"Is this writing in my voice?"** Not yet: the stand-in cannot write. The
  measurement, the checks, the flags and the structure are real; the prose
  starts the day the model is connected.
- **"Can I get the image?"** Yes, you just did. Templated by design so a
  carousel is never misspelt; AI-generated backgrounds are a separate decision
  and cost (docs/client/ai-and-accounts-setup.md, decision 2).
- **"When can my clients log in?"** After the accounts in the setup note are
  opened: sign-in emails, storage and hosting are the three things left on our
  side. Say it plainly; do not demo the email flow beyond the "check your
  email" page.
- **Gmail connection**: show the Connections page and explain the read-once
  design; do not attempt a live connect without Google credentials.

## Things not to click

- "Suggest others" on Create more than once (it is a model call; fine on mock).
- Settings > Connections > Connect (no credentials in `.env`).
- Delete project, unless you type the name on purpose; Archive is reversible.
